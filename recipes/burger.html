Homemade burger

A simple and delicious homemade burger recipe that is perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 500g Ground beef
  • 1 Onion, finely chopped
  • 1 Egg
  • 1 Tsp Salt
  • 1 Tsp Black pepper
  • 4 Burger buns
  • Lettuce leaves
  • Tomato slices
  • Cheese slices (optional)

Steps

  1. Preheat the grill or a skillet over medium-high heat.
  2. In a large bowl, combine the ground beef, onion, egg, salt, and pepper. Mix well.
  3. Form the mixture into 4 patties.
  4. Grill the patties for about 5 minutes on each side, or until cooked through.
  5. Toast the burger buns on the grill or in a toaster.
  6. Assemble the burgers by placing a patty on each bun and adding your desired toppings.
