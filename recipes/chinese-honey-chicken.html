Chinese honey chicken

A sweet and salty fakeaway for all the family and saves a fortune on ordering it

Ingredients

  • 130g Baby corn
  • 5 Chicken breasts
  • 350g Diced peppers
  • 115g Frozen diced onions
  • 2 Tsp Garlic puree
  • 2Tsp Ginger puree
  • 120ml Runny honey
  • 180ml Soy sauce
  • 1 Tbsp Cornflour

Steps

  1. Cut the baby corn in half on the diagonal. Cut the chicken breasts into chunks. Add to a large mixing bowl with all the remaining ingredients. Mix well until coated.
  2. Pour the contents of the bowl into a large saucepan. Place on a medium heat, bring to the boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook for 25-30 minutes, stirring regularly, until the chicken is cooked through
